## Long version (launch post)

> **Day Reminders is live** 🎯
>
> A personal productivity tab + bot that lives inside Teams. Capture what you need to remember today (or any day), and the bot pings you in chat before each one. No more flipping to another app.
>
> **What you can do:**
> - Add reminders with a due date, time, client (auto-completes), and notes.
> - Get a proactive card in your Day Reminders chat when each one is due — with Mark done / Snooze / Tomorrow buttons.
> - End-of-day "are you done?" check-in at a time you set.
> - View your week at a glance, group by tag or client, filter by anything.
> - Quick-add from any Teams chat via the `...` menu under the message box.
> - Slash commands in the bot chat: `/add 5pm tomorrow #work Send report`.
>
> **Where to find it:** the alarm-clock icon in your Teams left rail (Day Reminders). Open the Chat tab once to wire up notifications, then mostly live in the Reminders tab.
>
> **Quick guide:** click the **?** button in the top-right of the tab any time, or check the full guide at `docs/USERGUIDE.md` in the repo.
>
> **Coming next:** sharing — assign reminders to teammates, set up per-tag default recipient lists (e.g. `#QC = [Benex, Tim]`).
>
> Bugs / requests → ping Joshua, or drop them in this channel.
