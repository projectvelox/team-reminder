                  Licenses tab, quick guide

                  Everything you can do on this tab, in one place. Press Esc or click Close to dismiss.

                  Views

                  Table / Calendar
                  Toggle in the top bar. Table is the default; Calendar shows a monthly grid of every license by its expiry date.
                  Group: None / Customer / Owner / Product
                  Groups the table into sections you can collapse or expand by clicking the section header. Useful when one customer or owner has lots of licenses.

                  Filtering

                  Quick chips
                  All / Mine / This month / Overdue / Needs attention. "Needs attention" surfaces rows stuck in a status, close to expiry, or past expiry without renewal.
                  Sidebar breakdowns
                  OWNER / PRODUCT / STATUS chips are multi-select. Click multiple values to filter on any of them. A "N selected · Clear" appears so you can reset a whole axis in one click.
                  Search
                  The search box in the top bar matches customer, license type, notes, product line, and owner name.

                  Actions on each row

                  Edit
                  Opens the edit drawer where you can change every field plus see the activity log of who changed what.
                  Email / Email N
                  Opens Outlook with a pre-filled renewal notice draft (one per row, or one combined message when the row is part of a bundle of N licenses for the same customer). Status is not changed automatically — move the row to Notice sent yourself once the email leaves your outbox.
                  Renew
                  Marks the license renewed with a 1, 2, or 3 year extension (or custom date). Triennial licenses default to +3 years.
                  ··· More
                  Delete this license. Comes with a 5-second Undo toast.
                  Customer name (clickable)
                  Click to open the Customer 360 drawer: all licenses for that customer, total seats, contact emails, and customer notes.

                  Workflow

                  Status pipeline
                  Each license walks through Not started → Notice sent → Awaiting customer → Customer confirmed → Renewed. Status changes are always explicit (Edit drawer or the bot's follow-up card) — drafting an email does not promote the row.
                  Bundle indicator
                  When a customer has 2+ licenses expiring within 14 days of each other, the Email button on every row in that bundle reads "Email N". One click drafts one combined message covering all N licenses; you then mark them as Notice sent once you actually send.
                  Daily morning briefing
                  Each weekday at 8 AM PH, the bot sends you a Teams card summarising your queue. Skip it from Settings.
                  Monthly email digest
                  First day of each month, you receive an email listing your renewals (overdue, this month, next month). Opt out from Settings.

                  Bulk operations

                  Select
                  Top-bar button toggles a checkbox column. Pick multiple rows, then reassign owner in one round-trip.
                  CSV menu
                  Import a CSV (with owner auto-match and update-or-skip mode), export the current filtered view, or download a template with example rows.
                  Templates
                  Edit per-product-line renewal email templates (M365, BC, BREP, etc.). Variables: {customer}, {customerFirstWord}, {licenseType}, {users}, {expiryDate}, {ownerName}.

                  Settings

                  Theme
                  Auto follows Teams; you can force Light / Dark / High contrast just for this tab.
                  Default lead time
                  How many days before expiry the bot reminds you (per-row override available in each license).
                  All-accounts roll-up digest
                  Sales-lead view: monthly digest also includes a section of every owner's renewals, not just yours.
                  Refresh
                  The Refresh button in the top bar reloads everything from the server without a full tab reload.

                    Privacy & data handling

                    What we store

                    • Your settings (lead times, quiet hours, EOD time, theme override).
                    • Your reminders (title, time, dates, tags, notes — only ever visible to you).
                    • Your saved license filter views.
                    • A Teams chat handle (conversation reference) so the bot can send you proactive cards. No tokens or message content.
                    • License rows that you create or are assigned as Owner of — shared with the rest of the Kation tenant, not deleted with your account.

                    Where it's stored

                    Azure Table Storage (Southeast Asia). Compute runs in East Asia. Authentication is Microsoft Entra (Teams SSO); we never see your password. All API responses set Cache-Control: no-store and X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff.

                    How long we keep it

                    • Active reminders & license rows: indefinitely, until you delete them.
                    • Soft-deleted license rows: 30 days, then permanently purged.
                    • Done reminders > 24h: hidden from the UI; storage retains them until you delete.
                    • License event log: last 50 events per row.
                    • License comments: last 30 per row.

                    Your controls

                    • Export my data — downloads a JSON of everything we store about you.
                    • Delete my account data — wipes your reminders, settings, templates, and conversation ref; unassigns you from any license rows you own.

                    Third parties

                    Microsoft Graph (to send Teams cards + the monthly email digest). No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no third-party tracking.
