Quick guide

  • Add tags: type #work or #urgent directly in the title, e.g. Send report #work.
  • Filter by tag: click any #chip to see only that tag. Press Esc to clear.
  • High priority: click the ☆ next to a row to pin it to the top.
  • Edit inline: click a title or time to change it. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
  • Reorder: grab the ☰ handle to drag items within Anytime today or tag sections.
  • More options: click ⋯ on a row to set a custom lead time (overrides your default).
  • Group by tag: the top-right toggle, or press g.
  • Mark done: tick the checkbox. Undo within 5 seconds.
  • Bot chat: open the Chat tab above and type /help, /list, /add, /done.
  • Shortcuts: / focus add, g group, ? this guide.

Settings

The bot will send an "Are you done?" card to your chat at this time on workdays.

"Match Teams" follows whatever theme you have in Teams. The others lock the appearance regardless of the host.
