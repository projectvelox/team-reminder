Welcome to Day Reminders

  1. Type a task above and press Add. Optional: pick a date, time, and client.
  2. Set a Client to track which engagement this is for. Autocompletes from your past clients; shows as a chip on each row and in the proactive card.
  3. Click + Details to add notes (links, context, sub-tasks) below the title.
  4. Add #tags in the title (e.g. Send report #work) to group your day.
  5. Open the Chat tab to get proactive nudges in Teams; type /help there for shortcuts.

Quick guide
  • Tags inline: type #work or #urgent right in the title, e.g. Send report #work. Click any #chip on a row to filter; Esc clears.
  • Client: set a client per reminder; autocompletes from past ones. Click the client chip on a row to filter to it; click again to clear.
  • Auto-roll: undone reminders carry forward to today (up to 30 days) with an overdue Nd badge.
  • Edit inline: click a title, date, time, or description on any row. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
  • Reorder: drag the ☰ handle (works within Anytime today, tag, and priority sections).
  • Per-row options: click ⋯ on a row for details, custom lead time, or to move to tomorrow.
  • Shortcuts: / add, f search, g group, v view, ? toggle this guide, Esc clear filters.

Settings

The bot will send an "Are you done?" card to your chat at this time on workdays.

"Match Teams" follows whatever theme you have in Teams. The others lock the appearance regardless of the host.
